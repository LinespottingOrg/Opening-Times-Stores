## Description (replace)
```
Opening Times EU is a lightweight Windows desktop widget that keeps your favourite shops’ opening hours and the local weather in one place.

See today’s hours at a glance, the full week, specials and sale alerts, and weather for Stora Frö, Kalmar, and Karleby (data from MET Norway / yr.no) without opening a browser.

Features:
• Favourite stores with today’s open/closed status
• Full week hours on each card
• “Closes in…” / “Opens in…” timers
• Specials / sale indicators
• Weather for three places, next 12 hours, sunrise and sunset
• Temperature in green at or above 0 °C, blue below zero
• Search and quick-add
• Dark and light theme

Designed for everyday errands. Pin the stores you actually use.
```
